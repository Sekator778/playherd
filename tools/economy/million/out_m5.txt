== leak per day of a skilled share, treasury view (BTC, L=10, 1000 PLAY stakes, 10 bets/day each) ==
a=0.52: gross edge +0.0278% of stake (treasury loses this per bet), fee 0.1020% of stake, net for the player -0.0742%
a=0.56: gross edge +0.0834% of stake (treasury loses this per bet), fee 0.1020% of stake, net for the player -0.0186%
a=0.6: gross edge +0.1389% of stake (treasury loses this per bet), fee 0.1020% of stake, net for the player +0.0369%
a=0.65: gross edge +0.2084% of stake (treasury loses this per bet), fee 0.1020% of stake, net for the player +0.1064%
If fees go to HERD the treasury bears the gross edge of every player with a>0.5 (and also of fee-losing players with a in (0.5, a*): they lose net but still take money out of T).
