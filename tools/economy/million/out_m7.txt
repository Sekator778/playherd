== 3. expected cost of moving X PLAY from one own account to the other by a random walk of pair bets (fees only) ==
To make account B end X above the pack by repeated opposite bets of stake S at leverage L on BTC: rounds n ~ (X/(S*L*E|r|))^2 (random walk step S*L*E|r|), fees 2*n*fee(S,L).
X=10000 S=10000 L=10: step 69.5 PLAY/round, rounds ~ 2.07e+04, fees ~ 3.85e+05 PLAY = 38.54x the amount moved  (cost/gain >1 means the farm loses money)
X=10000 S=10000 L=100: step 694.7 PLAY/round, rounds ~ 207, fees ~ 3.82e+04 PLAY = 3.82x the amount moved  (cost/gain >1 means the farm loses money)
X=10000 S=250 L=1200: step 208.4 PLAY/round, rounds ~ 2.3e+03, fees ~ 1.2e+04 PLAY = 1.20x the amount moved  (cost/gain >1 means the farm loses money)
X=5000 S=250 L=240: step 41.7 PLAY/round, rounds ~ 1.44e+04, fees ~ 7.48e+04 PLAY = 14.96x the amount moved  (cost/gain >1 means the farm loses money)
